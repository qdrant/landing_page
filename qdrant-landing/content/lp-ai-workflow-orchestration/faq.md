  - id: 0
    question: How do I get started with Qdrant Cloud?
    answer: Sign up and create a free cluster in the Console, with no credit card. Then index your first vectors and run a filtered search.
  - id: 1
    question: How do I build an AI application on Qdrant?
    answer: Store your data as vectors with payload fields, then call Qdrant from each step of your app that needs context. The Cloud quickstart takes you from a free cluster to your first query.
  - id: 2
    question: Where does Qdrant fit in AI workflow orchestration?
    answer: Qdrant is the retrieval layer your orchestrator calls. Use CrewAI, LlamaIndex, Haystack, LangChain, or AutoGen to run the workflow, and Qdrant to store and search the vectors each step needs.
