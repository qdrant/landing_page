questions:
  - id: 0
    question: How do I get started with Qdrant Cloud?
    answer: A free Qdrant Cloud cluster has 1 GB RAM, 0.5 vCPU, 4 GB disk, and one node, enough for about 1 M vectors of 768 dimensions, with no credit card. Move to a Standard cluster when you need dedicated resources, multi-node high availability, backups, and SLAs.
  - id: 1
    question: Do I need to host an embedding model?
    answer: On Qdrant Cloud, Cloud Inference generates embeddings server-side from the text or image you send. You can also bring vectors from any model you already use.
  - id: 2
    question: Can I combine semantic search with keyword search?
    answer: Yes. Hybrid search runs semantic and keyword search in a single query and returns results that match the meaning, the exact terms, or both.
