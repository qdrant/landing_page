Recommended for your team
Qdrant Hybrid Cloud

Your data residency or VPC requirements rule out standard managed cloud, but you'd rather not build vector database operations in-house. Hybrid Cloud gives you managed operations inside your own infrastructure.

  • Managed database operations within your own VPC
  • Data stays in your cloud, which meets residency and compliance mandates
  • Deploys via a Kubernetes Operator into a cluster you already run
  • Automatic scaling and optimization, infrastructure control retained
Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung runs Hybrid Cloud inside its own Azure Kubernetes Service environment, indexing over 14 million vectors across decades of archive with a target of 40 to 50 million. Read the case study
