FAQ

Will my Chroma client code work with Qdrant?

No. Qdrant has its own SDKs (Python, TypeScript, Rust, Go, .NET, Java), so your query code changes.

Do I have to re-embed my data?

No. The migration tool copies your dense embeddings, metadata, and document text as they are.

How do you keep tenants isolated?

Each point carries a tenant ID, and a tenant-indexed payload filter scopes every query.

Can you meet our security review?

Qdrant holds SOC 2 Type II, signs a HIPAA BAA on Qdrant Managed Cloud, and provides a GDPR Data Processing Agreement. Reports and policies are at qdrant.tech/security.
